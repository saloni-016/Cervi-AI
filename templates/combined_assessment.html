Combined Assessment

Enter your clinical information and upload a cervical-cell image in one workflow. CerviAI will display the outputs from its clinical model and image model separately.

Important: This is an experimental educational prototype, not a clinically validated test or a medical diagnosis. The clinical estimate and image-classification confidence measure different things. Neither can confirm or rule out cancer. Discuss screening with a qualified healthcare professional.

01 — Clinical Information

Enter the same health-history details used in your existing Clinical Risk Assessment.

02 — Cervical-Cell Image

Upload a supported BMP, JPG, JPEG, or PNG image. The model will predict one of its learned cell categories.
