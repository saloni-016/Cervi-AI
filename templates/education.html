Short introductions to common cervical health topics. Confirm personal decisions with a qualified clinician and current official guidance.

01 / BASICS
◎

What is cervical screening?

Screening looks for certain HPV infections or cell changes before symptoms appear. The recommended test and timing depend on local guidance and personal circumstances.

Learn from: WHO · ICMR · ACS official guidance
02 / HPV
✳

Understanding HPV

Human papillomavirus is common. Some types can cause cervical cell changes. A clinician can explain what a test result means and whether follow-up is needed.

Learn from: WHO · ICMR · ACS official guidance
03 / VACCINATION
♡

HPV vaccination

HPV vaccines help prevent infection from important cancer-causing HPV types. Vaccination does not remove the need for screening when screening is recommended.

Learn from: WHO · ICMR · ACS official guidance
04 / PREPARATION
⌁

Preparing for an appointment

Ask what the test involves, share concerns, and request that the clinician explain each step. Follow the clinic's instructions about preparation.

Ask the clinic for test-specific instructions
05 / MYTHS
?

Questions without judgement

It is okay to feel nervous or have questions. A healthcare professional can discuss privacy, discomfort, consent, and available options.

Bring your questions to a trusted clinician
06 / NEXT STEP
↗

When to seek care

Unusual bleeding, persistent unusual discharge, or other concerning symptoms deserve medical evaluation. Do not wait for a screening tool to decide whether to seek care.
